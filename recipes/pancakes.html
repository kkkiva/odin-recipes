Pancakes

Back to Recipes-Odin

Description

This is my recipe for the best pancakes in the world. The recipe works well with both savory and sweet fillings!

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 3 tablespoons of sugar
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1/2 cup of flour
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 1 cup of boiling water

Method

  1. Mix the eggs, salt and sugar

    Crack 3 eggs into a bowl. Add the salt and sugar. Mix everything together.

  2. Add the milk and flour

    Add 1 cup of milk and 1/2 cup of flour. Mix until the batter is smooth.

  3. Add the remaining ingredients

    Add the second cup of milk and vegetable oil. Mix everything together.

  4. Add boiling water

    Slowly add 1 cup of boiling water while mixing the batter.

  5. Cook the pancakes

    Heat a frying pan and lightly grease it with vegetable oil. Pour a thin layer of batter into the pan and cook on both sides until golden.
