Recipes-Odin

Description

Ingredientss

For the dough

  • All-purpose flour – 3 cups
  • Egg – 1 pc
  • Cold water – 1/2 cup
  • Salt – 1/2 tsp
  • Vegetable oil – 1 tbsp

For the filling

  • Ground pork – 400g
  • Ground beef – 200g
  • Onion – 1 pc, finely chopped
  • Garlic – 2 cloves, minced
  • Salt and black pepper – to taste
  • Cold water or milk – 2–3 tbsp to make the filling juicy

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Make the dough. Mix flour, salt, egg, oil, and water. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Make the fillingCombine pork, beef, onion, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  3. ShapeRoll out the dough thin (2 mm). Cut into small circles. Place 1 tsp of meat in the center, fold in half, seal edges, and join the corners.
  4. Cook Drop into boiling salted water. Once they float, cook for 5–7 minutes.
  5. Serve Serve hot with butter and sour cream.
